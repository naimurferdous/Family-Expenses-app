Wallet brand logos (optional)

Put the official logo files here and the app shows them automatically next to the wallet name:
  bkash.png   (or bkash.svg)
  nagad.png   (or nagad.svg)
  rocket.png  (or rocket.svg)
  upay.png    (or upay.svg)

Square images with a transparent or white background work best (about 128x128 px).
Get the official files from each brand's own website / press kit. Without a file the app shows a
brand-colour letter badge (bKash pink, Nagad orange, Rocket purple, Upay yellow).
